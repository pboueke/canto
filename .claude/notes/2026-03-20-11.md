## Carousel centering fix

- Root cause: horizontal FlatList with `pagingEnabled` doesn't respect parent container's right padding, causing images to touch the right edge
- Fix: replaced `pagingEnabled` with `snapToInterval` + `decelerationRate="fast"` for precise snap control
- Added `contentContainerStyle={{ paddingHorizontal: 10 }}` for equal padding on both sides
- Added `marginRight` spacing between carousel items
- Reduced IMAGE_WIDTH to account for internal FlatList padding
